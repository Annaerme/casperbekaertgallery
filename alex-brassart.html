Alex Brassart

Painting · b. 2000

Gallery in the Distillery 2026 · Gallery in the Bakery 2025

Alex Brassart (born 2000) is a contemporary artist who works with fragments sourced from films, documentaries, found images, and television broadcasts.

His paintings carry a subtle, restless atmosphere and open up spaces for interpretation. They create an interplay between the image, the past, and the viewer, with the elusive and intangible at the core.
